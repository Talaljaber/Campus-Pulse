## Reading
The top five by tune (Gemini, GLM flash, DeepSeek, Qwen flash, GLM flagship) are within noise (0.873-0.883). On check DeepSeek is first. DeepSeek is the cheapest per unit of score (0.045 USD per 1000 mixed requests), the fastest (p50 about 0.5 s), reasoning-free, and beats the baseline on both splits. Runner-up: gemini-3.5-flash-lite (best tune score, most reproducible, but about 5x the cost and mandatory reasoning). Translation text varies between runs (31 % of DeepSeek translations changed at temperature 0) but the downstream F1 barely moves; extraction changed 9 %, tie-break 0 %.
