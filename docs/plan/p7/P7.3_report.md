Scope: raise-only `high_floor` families in `config/lexicons/urgency.yaml`, `UrgencyFloorRule`, the `urgency_rule`
floor in `PriorityFinalizer`, and Arabizi `info_request` start patterns. The distress floor (decision D-B) was

## Head diagnostic (`LogisticPriorityHead.coefficients()`, v1)

The head's inputs are p_low, p_medium, p_high from the ensemble (each in [0, 1]) plus ten 0/1 evidence flags.
For the class `high`: p_high +0.56, p_low -0.47, p_medium -0.15; evidence: deadline_today +2.80, enrollment_blocked
+2.77, stranded +2.34, safety_risk +2.14, exam_now +2.11, fault_report +1.03, info_request -0.56, ambient_fixture
-1.01. The whole probability block can swing the high logit by about 1.0; one evidence flag swings it by 2 to 2.8.
For the class `low`, info_request is +2.57 and ambient_fixture +2.71, while fault_report is -1.99. So the evidence
flags decide the priority when they fire; the model probabilities only decide when no flag fires. That is why
implicit urgency (no flag) falls back to the model's low/medium prior, and why a raise-only floor, not a
re-weighting, is the right tool here.

### models.md "## 36. Priority floors for implicit urgency (P7.3)"

Three raise-only families in `lexicons/urgency.yaml` (`high_floor:`), run by `UrgencyFloorRule` on the stripped
`model_text` after the injection guard: `account_compromise`, `deadline_24h` (time within 24 h AND a deadline word
AND a block cue), `major_outage` (outage AND building-wide scope, or outage AND an exam in progress). A hit makes
the priority one-hot high, floor `urgency_rule`, flag `urgency_floor:<family>`; precedence is distress (not
shipped) > safety > clarification answer > urgency rule > head. Tone, wrappers, injections and queue-jumping never
fire it. Gates: 0 hits on non-high official rows (11 hits, all on high rows), OOF
priority 0.9036 unchanged, our_test unchanged (cat 0.868, pri 0.847, 0 non-high hits). Dev tune priority macro-F1
0.719 -> 0.813, implicit-urgency high recall 0.48 -> 0.81; dev check 0.609 -> 0.700 and 0.29 -> 0.52 (check
reported once). The head's evidence weights (about 2 to 2.8 logits per flag against about 1.0 for the whole model
block) are why a floor, not a retrain, is used. Also: six Arabizi `info_request` start patterns (zero official
hits).
