## Known gaps and follow-ups

1. Precedence: `SafetyGuard` returns `distress` before `danger`, so a request with both loses the `danger` verdict and
   the safety tag. Documented by `test_distress_wins_over_danger_when_both_match`. Follow-up: let the verdict carry both.
   With P7.3 D-B the priority is still high.
2. A flood or burst pipe with no electrical or slip cue (a tune row of this kind) is not danger. Left out on purpose.
3. Outage, account compromise and deadline_24h are P7.3's floors, not lexicon danger.
4. Arabizi patterns are written for common spellings only; the encoder-side translit is noisy (gaz becomes "قاز"),
   so Arabizi patterns match the original half. Dev rows are LLM-authored, not native-verified.
5. The lexicon is snapshotted into `models/frozen/v1/lexicons`, so none of this is live until the lead refreezes.
