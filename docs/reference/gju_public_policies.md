## Programs, credit hours, fees (School of Computing)
- CS bachelor: 145 credit hours (tracks: General, Data Science, Cybersecurity; each can add Dual Studies).
- CE bachelor: 176 credit hours (tracks: General, AI & ML, Computer Vision & Robotics; each with Dual Studies).
- Fees: JD 100 per credit hour (bachelor CS/CE), JD 120 (CE master).
- Financial assistance: Ministry of Higher Education scholarship, student employment, project grants.
- Sources: https://www.gju.edu.jo/content/computer-engineering-faqs-14471 · https://www.gju.edu.jo/content/faqs-14844

## Field training in Jordan (CS)
- 160 hours at a Jordanian IT company (8 h/day × 4 weeks or 4 h/day × 8 weeks).
- Requires ≥ 90 credit hours and written email approval from the CS department chair.
- Not during an enrolled semester; mandatory before the German Year.
- Source: https://www.gju.edu.jo/content/faqs-14844

## German Year (German Year Manual, 2022)
- Mandatory year in Germany: a study semester + an internship semester (≥ 20 weeks full-time, 12 CH).
- Outgoing list requires GPA ≥ 61%. Before travelling: GPA ≥ 60%, obligatory courses passed, field training done. Minimum passed CH: Engineering 90; CS, Management, Design 85.
- Choose up to 5 partner universities in MyGJU; do not contact hosts directly (the International Affairs Department handles it).
- Learning Agreement signed before departure; German Year courses are pass/fail and not in the GJU GPA.
- Internship contract must be approved in MyGJU before starting; certificate + report within 2 months of the next semester.
- Blocked account 11,361 EUR / 12 months (proof by 10 June or 10 December); health insurance ~110 EUR/month; living ~700–1,000 EUR/month.
- Source: https://www.gju.edu.jo/sites/default/files/08.08.22_german_year_manual.pdf
