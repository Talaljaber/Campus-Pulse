# AI QUEST 2026 — FIX MY CAMPUS!
### German Week × School of Computing Day | Monday, 16 November 2026

**Build an AI system that understands student requests and helps route them to the right campus service.**

Calling all School of Computing students, from first year to final year! Join a team of **2–3 students**, choose a challenge track, and solve a real-world-style problem using AI/ML. Beginner and advanced teams are welcome.

**Track A — AI Starter:** Classify student requests into six service categories.
**Track B — AI Builder:** Classify the requests and predict their priority (Low, Medium, High).
**Track C — AI Innovator:** Deliver a working interactive demo, robustness checks, and an explanation of predictions, in addition to Track B.

**Important:** All tracks compete in **separate track rankings**, with a common 100-point rubric adjusted for the track; advancement and overall award categories are announced in the official rules.

**Key dates**
- Sunday 25 October: Announcement and registration open
- Thursday 29 October: Challenge brief and dataset released to registered teams
- Tuesday 10 November, 23:59 Jordan time: Submission deadline
- 11–13 November: External or independent judges evaluate submissions remotely
- Monday 16 November: Winners announced at School of Computing Day

**Submission:** Working code/notebook, reproducible instructions, predictions file, 2-page technical report, and a demo video (maximum 3 minutes). Track C must include a runnable application.
